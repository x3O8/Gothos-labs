# Gothos Labs

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

Web: independent static marketing site, with Arogya ELogBook as the flagship product.

## Stack

Buildless HTML, CSS, classic JavaScript, inline SVG, locally bundled fonts, and WebGL2. GhostFibers from the user's supplied text is adapted to a plain JavaScript lifecycle; React is not required.

## Product Purpose

Introduce Gothos Labs as a company creating medical solutions with a thoughtful touch of AI, as clarified by the user. Arogya ELogBook is its flagship product, not the company's entire scope. Company-level copy reflects that hierarchy; ELogBook product sections retain their concrete training focus. No specific AI functionality is claimed for ELogBook and no additional products are invented.

## Product Evidence

Copy and interface structure were checked against the local application's Dashboard.tsx, AppLayout.tsx, LoginProductPreview.tsx, and index.css. The source confirms dashboard insights, department-defined completion targets, case and procedure logs, academic activities, postings and rotations, assessments, thesis/certification records, leave records/approvals, faculty evaluation queues, and a printable logbook. Feature availability can vary by department configuration.

## Brand and Visual Direction

Retain the supplied video's dark/lime composition and section sequence. The hero now presents a dark resident dashboard and navigable ELogBook screens instead of a workflow diagram. Use the user-supplied GhostFibers background: dotted, twisting ribbons, depth of field, bloom, mouse-responsive fibers, and click pulses. The background rotates with page scroll and tilts/parts around the mouse, while respecting pause and reduced motion. Configure lime and teal colors to fit the existing site.

## Scope and Constraints

Keep this project outside Electronic-LogBook. The application source is read only for product evidence and visual reference; application code, databases, permissions, patient records, and repository state are not changed or accessed. Do not display real identifiers, patient text, invented metrics, testimonials, pricing, or unprovided contact addresses.

## Preview Behavior

The hero has six screens: Dashboard, Postings & Rotations, Case Logs, Procedure Logs, Academic Activities, and Assessments. It can expand into a dialog. Its frosted glass surface lets the background show through, and a slight Y-axis tilt follows the reference video. The screens are a dark interpretation of the existing layout and labels, not a live authenticated embed or screenshot. No clinical records or fabricated training totals are displayed. Empty preview states are intentional.

## Enquiries

The form downloads a Gothos Labs enquiry brief locally, with topics for medical software, AI collaboration, and Arogya ELogBook. Product actions preselect the ELogBook topic by value. It does not send a message. A contact destination and delivery integration remain needed for actual submission.


## Presentation refinement

Page content uses staggered, one-time viewport fade-ins, with immediate visibility for keyboard focus, pause, and reduced motion. A large Gothos Labs wordmark anchors the footer and returns visitors to the top.

## Annotated company revision

Headline: Built for medicine. Made for people. The header training badge and hero announcement badge are removed. The small duplicate footer brand link is removed; the large footer wordmark remains. The company-level about, enquiry, and footer copy now reflect medical solutions and AI.
